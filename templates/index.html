How RankPath scores a college

  1. Looks up the last 3 years of MCC AIQ Round 1 closing ranks for that course and your category.
  2. Computes a weighted closing rank: 50% latest year, 30% prior, 20% prior-prior.
  3. Compares your rank to that weighted number and assigns a bucket: Safe, High, Borderline, Low, Unlikely.
  4. Every prediction shows the three historical numbers that drove the bucket. No black box.
